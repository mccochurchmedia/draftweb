Welcome

New here? We were too, once.

Methodist Congregational Church of Ontario started as a handful of Filipino families meeting in a small church. Today we're a big church, but the heart is the same — real people, real questions, and a faith that has room for both. Our Sunday service runs alongside a Kids Service.

Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

We are an open group, inclusive, and Christ-centered. We seek to grow in faith, in love, and help build a better community. The majority of our members are younger families with growing children, along with young adults, youth, and a few elderly members. What makes this group unique is our harmonious relationships — open to one another, vibrant, and full of the spirit of unity. In our closeness as one big family, our fellowship has been given the identity, “Familia Ragragsac.”

Our Mission

To love God, love people, and grow together as a family of faith that welcomes everyone exactly as they are.

Our Vision

A church where every generation, background, and story has a place — building a community that reflects God's love in Toronto and beyond.

Who We Welcome

We seek to connect with new migrants, with those who have no active church affiliation, and with fellow believers of the same Christian background who are looking for a church fellowship where they can grow in faith and belong as a Christian family — somewhere that feels good to be.

Get Involved

Find your place here

However you connect best — with kids, in conversation, or out in the community — there's a group for you.

Every group makes room for fellowship, and every group has a place in the choir.

Kids Ministry

A dedicated Kids Service runs alongside our 4:00pm Sunday service, for infants through 5th grade.

Youth Group

A weekly gathering for middle and high schoolers — games, discussion, and mentorship.

Young Adults

A community for college-age and twenty-somethings — casual meetups, deep conversations, and figuring out life together.

Adults

A steady community for adults of all stages — Bible study, prayer groups, and life-on-life support.

Seniors

A warm gathering for our senior members — fellowship, shared meals, and support for one another.

Let's Connect

A space to meet new people, catch up with familiar faces, and feel like part of the family — everyone's invited to connect.

Online Midweek Devotion

A short devotion held online in the middle of the week to keep us grounded in the Word between Sundays.

Counselling (1-on-1)

A private, one-on-one space with our pastoral team to talk through whatever you're carrying.

Visitation

Our team visits members at home or in the hospital, bringing prayer and presence when it's needed most.

Elderly Home Ministry

Twice a month, we visit a local elderly home to offer devotion and hymn singing for the residents and staff.

First Time Here?

Here's what to expect

A few honest answers to what people usually wonder before their first visit.

01

Come as you are

Jeans, a suit, whatever's comfortable — nobody's going to look twice. We just care that you're here.

02

Easy to find, easy to park

Free lot on-site, right at 25 Old York Mills Rd. Tap "Open in Google Maps" above and it'll walk you in.

03

Kids and youth are covered

A dedicated Kids & Youth Service runs alongside our Sunday service, so the whole family is looked after.

04

No pressure, ever

You won't be put on the spot or asked to stand up and introduce yourself. Just come, listen, and see how it feels.

05

Stay for fellowship

Service wraps up with fellowship time — coffee, snacks, and a chance to meet people at your own pace.
